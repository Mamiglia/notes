Can we employ model stitching and the platonic representation hypothesis to train models in environments where there is huge scarcity of labeled data?

In particular we need:
- a decent amount of unlabeled data, of unspecified modality X
- a pretrained LLM
- a small portion of labeled data of the kind text-to-X

Can we improve/develop T2X more than we can by just training on the labeled data?
