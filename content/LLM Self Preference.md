The authors show an interesting phenomenon where they show an LLM-Judge two different summaries of the same text and ask for a preference. The LLM consistenly prefers the summary generated by its own model. 

Furthermore they also show that the models are actively able to self-recognize themselves and show that self-recognition and self-preference are correlated.

The introduction of the paper is wonderfully written but the choice of metrics and plots is mediocre and fails to communicate the message efficiently.
